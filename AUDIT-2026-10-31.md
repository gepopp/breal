**Verdict legend**
- **Confirmed** – the problem exists (code or staging checked)
- **Likely** – plausible and consistent with the code, not reproduced by us
- **Fixed** – already solved in the code; the audit looked at an older state or interpreted it differently
- **Fixed locally** – solved in commit `3a3e2cf`, not yet deployed to staging
- **Content** – needs text/data from Ronald or the customer, not a code fix
- **Decision** – needs a customer/team decision first
- **Check** – we could not verify it ourselves; needs a test
